Fact 1: The amazing Octopus

The octopus has three hearts, nine brains, and blue blood. It can also change color and texture to blend in with its surroundings.

How smart are octopuses?

Octopuses are incredibly intelligent creatures. They can solve complex problems, use tools, and even show signs of playfulness.

Fact 2: lightning strikes

Lightning strikes the Earth about 100 times every second. It is a powerful natural phenomenon that can reach temperatures hotter than the surface of the sun.

How hot is lightning?

Lightning can reach temperatures of up to 30,000 Kelvin (53,540 degrees Fahrenheit), which is hotter than the surface of the sun.

Can lightning strike twice?

Yes, lightning can strike the same place multiple times. In fact, tall structures like skyscrapers and trees are often struck by lightning more than once.

Fact 3: The deep ocean

The deep ocean is the largest habitat on Earth, covering over 70% of the planet's surface. It is home to a vast array of unique and fascinating creatures.
